Notable troubleshooting: promoting the second domain controller kept failing with an "unknown error". Ruling out causes one by one, I found it needed a reboot after installing the AD DS role and domain-qualified credentials (DOMAIN\Administrator) instead of a local account. In another lab I diagnosed a USN rollback and a DNS misconfiguration.
